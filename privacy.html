Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 (replaces the version of October 11, 2026)

This Privacy Policy explains how Kresna Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Riftborn Legion TD (the "Game"), an Android mobile game published on Google Play.

1. Your game account

When you first play online, the Game automatically creates a guest account for you through Firebase Authentication. This account has a random user ID (UID) and does not ask for your name, email or password.

You can choose to link your guest account to a Google account or to Google Play Games so you can keep your progress on another device. When you link, we receive from Google the account identifier needed to sign you in and, depending on the provider, your Google Play Games display name or the email address and name of the Google account you chose. Linking is optional; you can play without it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your UID, the sign-in providers linked to it (guest, Google, Google Play Games), and the identifiers and profile details those providers return, as described above.
  • Game progress and in-game data: heroes, items, currencies, stage and event progress, settings, chosen player name and avatar, in-game mail and reward history. This is stored on our servers so your progress is saved and protected, and a copy is kept on your device for offline play.
  • Purchase information: when you buy something in the Game, Google Play processes the payment. We receive the purchase details needed to deliver and verify the item (product, order identifier, purchase token, time and status). We never receive or store your card or other payment details.
  • Advertising information: if you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier (where permitted), IP address, device information and ad interactions to deliver, measure and protect ads.
  • Technical information: app version, device model, operating system version, language and IP address, which our servers receive when the Game connects to them and which we use to run the service, prevent abuse and fix problems.

The Game does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

3. How we use information

  • To create and sign in to your account and save your progress across devices.
  • To deliver purchases, rewards, events and in-game mail, and to keep the in-game economy fair.
  • To show rewarded ads you choose to watch and to grant their rewards.
  • To detect fraud, cheating and abuse, and to keep the service secure.
  • To fix bugs, answer support requests and improve the Game.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-party services

We use the following services provided by Google, which process information under their own terms and privacy policies (Google Privacy Policy):

  • Firebase Authentication — guest accounts and Google / Google Play Games sign-in.
  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run and Cloud Firestore) — our game servers and the database that stores your account and progress.
  • Google Play Games Services — optional Play Games sign-in.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases.
  • Google AdMob — rewarded advertisements. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Advertising and your choices

Ads in the Game are rewarded ads that you choose to watch. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before personalized advertising. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings.

6. Data storage, retention and security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may be located outside your country. We use access controls and encrypted connections (HTTPS) to protect it. No system is completely secure.

We keep your account and game data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is scheduled for removal after a 7-day grace period and then deleted from our active systems. We may keep a limited record of purchases and anti-fraud information for as long as needed to meet legal, tax, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations. Information processed by Google services is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the Game: open Profile → Account → Delete account, confirm twice, and type the confirmation word. Signing in again during the 7-day grace period cancels the deletion. If you cannot open the Game, you can request deletion by email. Full instructions: Delete your Riftborn Legion TD account.

You may also contact us to ask for access to or correction of your information.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
