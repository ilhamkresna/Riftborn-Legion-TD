Delete your Riftborn Legion TD account

Riftborn Legion TD is developed by Kresna Labs. You can delete your game account and its data in two ways.

Option 1 — inside the Game (fastest)

  1. Open Riftborn Legion TD and make sure you are online.
  2. Open your Profile and go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Continue.
  4. Type the confirmation word shown on screen and tap Delete my account.

Option 2 — by email

If you can no longer open the Game, email kresnalabs@gmail.com with the subject "Riftborn Legion TD account deletion" and include:

  • your UID (shown on the Profile screen), or
  • the Google account email or Google Play Games name you linked to the Game.

We may ask you to confirm that you own the account before we delete it. We process requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (UID) and its links to Google or Google Play Games.
  • Your game progress: heroes, items, currencies, stage and event progress, settings, player name, avatar and in-game mail.
  • Purchased items and currencies in the Game. Deleting your account does not refund purchases.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Deletion is scheduled with a 7-day grace period. Signing in again during that time cancels it; after that, the data is permanently removed from our active systems.
  • Limited purchase and anti-fraud records may be kept for as long as legally required for tax, accounting and fraud prevention.
  • Payment records held by Google Play are managed by Google under its own policies.

Uninstalling the Game does not delete your account. To remove the local copy on your phone as well, uninstall the Game after deleting your account.
