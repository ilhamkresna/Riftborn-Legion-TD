Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kresna Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Riftborn Legion TD (the "Game"), an Android mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not require you to create an account or sign in. However, the Game and integrated third-party services may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate location inferred from IP address, and technical identifiers where available.
  • Gameplay and analytics information: app launches, sessions, in-game events, gameplay progress, and general usage patterns.
  • Crash and diagnostic information: crash reports, performance metrics, and diagnostic data used to identify and resolve problems.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers (where permitted), ad interactions, and information used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Game progress information: gameplay progress, settings, or other game state that may be stored in Firebase services using an app-generated identifier rather than a login account.

Specific information collected depends on the features enabled, device settings, consent choices, and the configuration of the services described below.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the Game and maintain gameplay progress.
  • To understand gameplay and improve performance and user experience.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and maintain security.
  • To display and measure advertisements, subject to applicable choices and legal requirements.
  • To prevent abuse and protect the Game and its services.

3. Third-Party Services

We use services provided by Google, which may process information under their own privacy policies and terms:

  • Google AdMob: serves advertisements and may process device identifiers, ad interactions, and related data. See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy guidance.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: helps us understand app usage and gameplay events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: helps us diagnose crashes and stability issues.
  • Firebase database services: may store game progress and related game data to support game functionality.

For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Advertising and Your Choices

The Game includes advertisements through Google AdMob. Depending on your region, device settings, and applicable consent requirements, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You may be able to manage advertising preferences through your device settings and Google's advertising controls. Where required, we request consent before using data for purposes that require it.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

Game progress and other data may be stored using Firebase infrastructure. We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. No transmission or storage system is completely secure. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, subject to applicable requirements and service configurations. Retention periods for data processed by third-party providers may be governed by their settings and policies.

6. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

Because the Game does not require a login, we may need additional information to identify the game data associated with your installation. You may contact us at kresnalabs@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we control, where applicable. Please include the Game name and any in-game player identifier if one is available. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law. Some information held by third-party providers may be subject to their own policies and controls.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action. Age thresholds and additional protections may vary by jurisdiction.

8. International Data Processing

Information processed through Google services may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, in accordance with the providers' applicable practices and legal requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game and its services change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
