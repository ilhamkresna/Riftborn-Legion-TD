Riftborn Legion TD

A swarm is pouring out of the Rift, and only your Core stands between it and the world. Riftborn Legion TD is a semi-idle tower defense RPG in portrait for Android: your heroes fight automatically, you aim the volleys, and every battle is shaped by the skill cards you draft.

  • Lead a squad of heroes from Common to Legendary and upgrade them.
  • Draft skill cards every run: extra projectiles, chain lightning, burn, poison, freeze and element reactions.
  • Place side towers such as frost turrets, tesla coils, gravity beacons and a dragon nest.
  • Seven worlds and 70 stages, plus Endless, dungeons, raid bosses and a weekly world boss.
  • Play stages offline; Auto Run and idle rewards keep progress going.
